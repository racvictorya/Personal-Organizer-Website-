Phase 0 · Symbol Exploration

The Mark

Not an initial — an elegant minimalist symbol that represents the idea of the business: order, calm, and space. Each is a single thin-line form that embosses beautifully and scales to a favicon.

Six Symbol Concepts

Concept 01
The Threshold
An archway — the calm passage into an ordered, beautiful space. Architectural, timeless, welcoming.
Concept 02
The Fold
A single continuous line folding back on itself — folded linens, order brought from disorder. Direct nod to organizing.
Concept 03
The Calm
An open circle (ensō) — completeness, balance, and serenity. Quiet, meditative, premium.
Concept 04
The Order
Nested squares — spaces within spaces, everything in its place. Structured, precise, great for the medical side.
Concept 05
The Vessel
A simple vessel — holding, containing, keeping. The act of giving everything a home. Warm and human.
Concept 06
The Lines
Calm, ordered horizontals of varied length — neat shelves, folded stacks, balance. Minimal and clean.
