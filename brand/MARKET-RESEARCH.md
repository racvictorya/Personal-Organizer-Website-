## 1. The Real Target Bracket — Reframe

A $100K salary in NYC has only **$30,362 in actual spending power** (FOX 5 NY, 2025).
For professional organizing to be a comfortable purchase — not a stressful one — the real sweet spot is:

| Segment | Annual HHI | Profile |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary target** | $150K–$300K | NYC/NJ professionals, dual-income households, homeowners |
| **Premium target** | $300K–$1M+ | Finance, law, medicine, executives — home + medical offices |
| **Medical/clinical target** | Practice revenue $500K+ | Clinic owners, OR managers, practice administrators |

> **Action:** All branding, copy, and pricing signals should speak to the $150K–$300K primary bracket, with visual signals sophisticated enough to attract the $300K+ tier without alienating the primary.

## 3. Logo Analysis — Which Design Wins for $100K+ Clients

### The Three ORDÖ Logos (Client-Created)

---

#### ✅ LOGO 1 — Didone Serif + Calligraphic Swash *(Selected)*
*High-contrast serif wordmark, flowing underline swash, spaced geometric caps descriptor, warm blush background*

**Typography class:** Didone / High-contrast editorial serif

**Research-backed performance signals:**

- **+13% perceived quality** — Serif fonts are directly linked to a 13% increase in perceived quality over sans-serif alternatives *(Monotype)*
- **+9% reliability perception** — Serifs boost reliability scores by 9%, critical for a service business built on trust *(Monotype)*
- **0.05 second brand impression** — Luxury consumers form brand impressions in 0.05 seconds; the Didone makes that impression count *(Schweitzer Designs)*
- **Didone = premium pricing signal** — Didone fonts communicate exclusivity and premium value *before* consumers engage with content. "The typography itself signals premium positioning." *(YouWorkForThem)*
- **Script/calligraphic element** — 97% of luxury brands use script elements in their identity. Script activates neural pathways that respond to art and beauty and slows reading speed just enough to make people *pause and really see the brand name* *(Letterhanna Studio)*
- **"Quiet luxury" alignment** — The dominant 2024–2025 luxury trend is subtlety, craftsmanship, and timeless restraint over logos. ORDÖ's serif + swash is understated yet unmistakably premium — exactly the "quiet luxury" signal affluent consumers seek *(Trivision)*

**Target audience fit:**

| Audience | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Affluent NYC homeowners (women 35–55) | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Mirrors luxury brands they already buy |
| Dual-income professionals | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Editorial aesthetic = trusted, sophisticated |
| Short Hills / Westchester homeowners | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Aspirational yet warm |
| Medical practice owners | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Slightly fashion-forward but still authoritative |
| Corporate/administrative | ⭐⭐⭐ | Serif trust signal works; swash may feel too soft |

**Verdict:** **Best overall performer for the $150K–$300K primary target.** The Didone + swash combination is the fastest, most efficient visual shortcut to "this is a premium personal service I can trust with my home."

## 4. The "Quiet Luxury" Trend — Why It Matters for ORDÖ

> *"Quiet luxury branding prioritizes subtlety, craftsmanship, exclusivity, and timeless appeal over flashy logos."* — Trivision, 2025

High-net-worth consumers in 2024–2026 are actively **moving away** from loud, logo-heavy branding. They want:
- **Restraint** — the brand doesn't shout
- **Craft** — you can see the care in the details
- **Heritage signals** — something that feels like it has history and intentionality
- **Personalization** — it feels made for someone like them

**ORDÖ Logo 1 hits all four.** The Didone serif has 200 years of luxury heritage. The calligraphic swash signals handcraft. The warm blush background is intimate and personal. Nothing is loud.

## 6. Recommendations

### Logo System
| Use | Logo |
|---|---|
| Primary mark (website, cards, all collateral) | **Logo 1 — Didone Serif + Swash** |
| Medical/professional materials | **Logo 2 — Geometric linen** |
| Social media, website backgrounds | **Logo 3 sage** as a color/aesthetic reference |

### Name
- **Visual brand:** ORDÖ (umlaut as design signature)
- **Legal/SEO/domain:** ORDO
- **Website:** ordo.com or ordo.co or getordo.com

### Website Positioning
- Lead with the residential/lifestyle appeal (Logo 1 aesthetic)
- Use the sage palette for visual warmth and wellness signaling
- Address medical/professional separately within the site — clean, precise tone

### Pricing Signal
- Never show price on the homepage — consultations only
- Language like "bespoke," "tailored," "curated" activates the luxury psychology
- The $150K+ client is not price-sensitive; they are **trust-sensitive**
