Why Custom Scope

No two projects are the same.

Space matters

A 400 sq ft studio and a 3,000 sq ft home both deserve excellence — but they require fundamentally different investments of time, materials, and approach.

Goals matter

Decluttering a single room and redesigning your entire home's organizational system are different projects entirely. Your scope is scoped to your goal.

Environment matters

Residential and clinical environments require different expertise, materials, and compliance considerations. Both receive the same standard of care.

Common Questions

What to expect.

How does the consultation work?

We start with a complimentary consultation — either in person or via video — to understand your space, your challenges, and your goals. No commitment required. After the consultation, you'll receive a custom proposal with transparent pricing.

Do I need to be home during the organizing session?

It's up to you. Some clients prefer to be present for the first session; others hand over the keys and return to a transformed space. We work comfortably either way.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve New York City and Northern and Central New Jersey. We are available for travel engagements nationwide — medical facilities, high-end residential projects, and moves that take us beyond the tri-state area.

Do you source organizing products?

Yes. After assessing your space, we provide product recommendations and sourcing guidance. We can source items on your behalf or walk you through what to purchase before a session.

What makes ORDÖ different from other organizers?

Victorya's background in clinical healthcare environments gives ORDÖ a precision-first perspective that most organizing studios simply don't have. Every system we build is designed to function in real life — not just look beautiful on the day we leave.
