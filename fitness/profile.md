## Back-friendly program changes (early disc herniation)
Follow the doctor's guidance first. Stop and check with the doctor if there is pain shooting down a leg, numbness/tingling, or back pain that gets worse during or after a lift.
- Weeks 1-4: stay at RPE 6-7 (3-4 reps in reserve) on all lower-body and back-loading lifts; progress load slowly.
- Day 1: back squat -> goblet squat, then leg press or hack squat (back flat against pad) once comfortable. RDL -> light dumbbell RDL with a short range (to just below knees), neutral spine; skip if it aggravates. Cable crunch -> dead bug 3x8-10/side.
- Day 2: seated dumbbell shoulder press on a bench with back support; chest-supported row instead of seated cable row.
- Day 4: back extension -> bird dog 3x8/side (reintroduce bodyweight back extension later if pain-free). Hanging knee raise -> Pallof press 3x10-12/side. Hack squat or goblet squat fine; hip thrust and split squats fine at light loads.
- Day 5: one-arm dumbbell row with hand and knee supported on a bench.
- Avoid for now: heavy barbell back squat, conventional deadlift, good mornings, sit-ups/crunches, heavy loads carried overhead while standing.
- Brace before each rep (ribs down, tight midsection); exhale on the effort.
